NAME

support - help for Number Keyboard - Digits

SYNOPSIS

  1. Open the app. On your Home Screen it is called Numeric Keyboard.
  2. Tap Open Settings.
  3. Tap Keyboards, then turn on Numeric Keyboard.
  4. In any app, hold the globe key and pick Numeric Keyboard.

You can also find it under Settings, General, Keyboard, Keyboards. Tap the globe key again to go back to your usual keyboard.

Allow Full Access is optional. It is only used for haptics.

ERRORS

ESECURE The keyboard does not show up in a password field.

That is how iOS works. In password and phone-number fields, iOS uses its own keyboard. No added keyboard can appear there.

ENOGLOBE I do not see a globe key.

The globe key shows when more than one keyboard is turned on. Check that Numeric Keyboard is in the list under Settings, General, Keyboard, Keyboards.

ENOBUZZ I do not feel the keys tap.

Turn on Haptics in the app, then turn on Allow Full Access for the keyboard in Settings. iOS only plays keyboard haptics with Full Access on.

ENOTHEME I bought Pro Themes and they are gone.

Open the app, go to Pro Themes and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

EREFUND How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
