Support

Help for Number Keyboard - Digits: how to turn it on, common questions, and how to reach me.

TURN THE KEYBOARD ON

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards.
  3. Tap Add New Keyboard.
  4. Pick Number Keyboard - Digits from the list.

To use it, open any app and tap a text field. Then touch and hold the globe key and choose the number keyboard. Tap the globe key again to go back to your usual keyboard.

COMMON QUESTIONS

The keyboard does not show up in a password field.

That is how iOS works. In password and other secure fields, iOS always uses its own keyboard. No added keyboard can appear there.

I do not see a globe key.

The globe key shows when more than one keyboard is turned on. Check that the keyboard is in the list under Settings, General, Keyboard, Keyboards.

I bought Pro Themes and they are gone.

Open the Number Keyboard - Digits app and use Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data does the app collect?

None. See the privacy policy.

CONTACT

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and I will answer there.

It helps to include your device, your iOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong. Please leave out anything private.
