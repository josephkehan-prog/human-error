Privacy Policy

Number Keyboard - Digits does not collect your data. This page says what that means in plain terms.

Effective October 12, 2026

SUMMARY

  • The app collects no data about you.
  • The keyboard has no network code. It cannot send what you type anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code in the app.

WHAT YOU TYPE

What you type goes to the app you are typing in, the same as with the built-in keyboard. The keyboard has no network code, so it has no way to send your typing to me or to anyone else. That is true whether Allow Full Access is on or off.

SETTINGS

Your choices in the app, such as the theme you picked, are saved on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

PURCHASES

Pro Themes is an optional one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment through the App Store. I never see your name, your card or your Apple Account details. Apple's handling of that purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

THIS WEBSITE

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. If you swap the screen color, that choice is saved in your own browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

CHILDREN

The app is rated 4+. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

CONTACT

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support page.
