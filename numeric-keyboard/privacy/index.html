NAME

privacy - what Number Keyboard - Digits knows about you (nothing)

SYNOPSIS

  • The app collects no data about you.
  • The keyboard has no network code. It cannot send what you type anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code in the app.

Effective October 12, 2026

WHO

Human Error is the name J. Han publishes apps under. It is one person. When this page says "I", that is who it means.

WHAT YOU TYPE

What you type goes to the app you are typing in, the same as with the built-in keyboard. The keyboard has no network code, so it has no way to send your typing to me or to anyone else.

FULL ACCESS

Allow Full Access is optional. The keyboard uses it for one thing: haptics, the small tap you feel when you press a key. iOS only lets a keyboard play haptics when Full Access is on.

You can leave it off and still type. On or off, the keyboard has no network code and collects nothing.

SETTINGS

Your choices in the app, such as theme, layout and sounds, are saved on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

PURCHASES

Pro Themes is an optional one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment through the App Store. I never see your name, your card or your Apple Account details. Apple's handling of that purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

THIS WEBSITE

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. If you swap the screen color, that choice is saved in your own browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email me, I get your email address and whatever you wrote. I use it to answer you and for nothing else.

CHILDREN

The app is rated 4+. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
