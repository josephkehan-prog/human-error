NAME

support - help for Whiteboard - Draw & Mind Map

SYNOPSIS

Whiteboard works on files. Pick a folder, such as one in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone, and your drawings are saved there as .excalidraw files.

You can open, move, rename and back up those files in the Files app like any other file.

ERRORS

ENOENT I cannot find a drawing.

Open the Files app and look in the folder you saved it to. If it is in iCloud Drive, give it a moment to finish downloading.

ESYNC A change did not show up on my other device.

iCloud Drive does the syncing. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud Drive is on, then wait a minute.

EFORMAT Which files can it open?

.excalidraw files, and .excalidraw.md files from a notes vault. Whiteboard saves them back in the same format.

EREFUND How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

EPRIVACY What data does the app collect?

None. See the privacy policy.
