Whiteboard - Draw & Mind Map 1.0

Hand-drawn diagrams and notes. COMING SOON

NAME

whiteboard - sketches, diagrams and mind maps that stay plain files

SYNOPSIS

whiteboard [--draw] [--diagram] [--mindmap] [--pencil] [--export]

DESCRIPTION

Most drawing apps keep your work inside the app. Whiteboard keeps it in a file you own.

It opens .excalidraw files from iCloud Drive or any folder you pick, and it saves them back to the same place. Nothing gets imported, and nothing gets locked in.

It is written in Swift for iPhone and iPad. No web view, no server, no account.

OPTIONS

--draw
Freehand lines, shapes, arrows and text on an endless canvas, with that hand-drawn look.
--diagram
Shapes snap into line and arrows stay attached when you move things.
--mindmap
Tab adds a child idea, Return adds a sibling.
--pencil
Apple Pencil ink that keeps up with your hand.
--export
PNG, SVG or PDF when you need to share a picture instead of the file.

Still switches in the app, not real flags.
