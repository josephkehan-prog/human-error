Whiteboard - Draw & Mind Map collects no data about you.

Effective November 11, 2026

NAME

privacy - what Whiteboard - Draw & Mind Map knows about you (nothing)

SYNOPSIS

  • The app collects no data about you.
  • Your drawings stay in the files and folders you choose. I never see them.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code in the app.

WHO

Human Error is the name J. Han publishes apps under. It is one person. When this page says "I", that is who it means.

YOUR DRAWINGS

Whiteboard saves your work as .excalidraw files in the place you pick, such as iCloud Drive, On My iPhone or another folder the Files app can reach. There is no Human Error server, so your drawings are never sent to me.

If you keep your files in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs them between your devices. That is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

When you share or export a drawing, it goes where you send it and nowhere else.

SETTINGS

Your choices in the app, and the list of folders you have opened, are saved on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

PURCHASE

Whiteboard is a one-time purchase on the App Store. Apple handles the payment. I never see your name, your card or your Apple Account details. Apple's handling of that purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

THIS WEBSITE

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. If you swap the screen color, that choice is saved in your own browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email me, I get your email address and whatever you wrote. I use it to answer you and for nothing else.

KEEPING AND DELETING

The app keeps no data about you, so I have nothing of yours to delete.

Your drawings are your files. Deleting the app does not delete them. Delete them in the Files app if you want them gone.

If you emailed me and want that email deleted, ask and I will delete it.

CHILDREN

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
